# Geothority Ad Video Intro Brief

## Goal
Create a short ad that introduces Geothority as the fastest way for local service businesses, especially insurance agents, to understand and improve their local visibility on Google, Maps, and AI search.

## Core promise
Geothority scans your local presence, shows you what is broken, and helps get the highest-impact fixes handled for you.

## Audience
- insurance agents
- multi-location local businesses
- owners paying for SEO but lacking clarity
- teams who know rankings matter but do not want another complicated tool

## Primary pain points
- unclear why competitors outrank them locally
- inconsistent listings across directories
- weak or missing city pages and schema
- poor visibility in Google Maps and AI answers
- no simple monitoring workflow after fixes are made

## Primary benefits
- fast Trust Stack scan in about 90 seconds
- clear prioritization instead of generic SEO reports
- visibility across Google, Maps, directories, reviews, and AI platforms
- schema, local content, and listing workflows from one dashboard
- weekly monitoring and competitor tracking

## 30-second hero ad concept
### Format
- 9:16 vertical
- 30 seconds
- platform: Meta Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts

### Hook options
1. Your local SEO is probably losing customers right now.
2. If Google Maps is quiet, your authority stack may be broken.
3. Most local businesses do not need more SEO noise, they need a clear fix list.
4. Want to know why competitors keep showing up before you?

### Script
**0-3s**
On-screen: "Your local SEO is probably losing customers right now."
Voiceover: "If Google Maps is quiet, your authority stack may be broken."

**3-8s**
On-screen: Geothority dashboard / scan animation
Voiceover: "Geothority scans your business across Google, directories, reviews, and AI platforms in about 90 seconds."

**8-14s**
On-screen: Trust Stack score + problem areas highlighted
Voiceover: "It shows you exactly what is broken, what matters most, and what to fix first."

**14-21s**
On-screen: schema wizard, content generation, citation syncing, competitor tracking
Voiceover: "From schema and city pages to listings, monitoring, and AI visibility, everything lives in one system."

**21-27s**
On-screen: weekly alerts / competitor movement / green score lift
Voiceover: "Then it keeps watch, so you spot changes before rankings slip."

**27-30s**
On-screen CTA: "Get your free scan"
Voiceover: "Get your free Geothority scan and see what is holding you back."

## 15-second cutdown
**Hook:** "Still guessing why competitors outrank you locally?"
**Body:** "Geothority scans your business across Google, Maps, directories, reviews, and AI. Then it shows you what is broken and what to handle first."
**CTA:** "Get your free scan in 90 seconds."

## Visual direction
- dark premium dashboard visuals
- bright emerald and teal highlights for key wins
- show simplified Trust Stack score, not every metric at once
- keep UI crops clean and uncluttered
- use 1-2 key proof points per scene, not dense walls of text

## CTA variants
- Get your free scan
- See your Trust Stack score
- Find out what is holding you back
- See why competitors outrank you

## A/B test angles
### Angle A: clarity
"Stop guessing. See what is broken in 90 seconds."

### Angle B: lost demand
"If you are not showing up in Maps and AI, customers are choosing someone else."

### Angle C: competitive urgency
"Your competitors are not waiting. Your local SEO should not either."
